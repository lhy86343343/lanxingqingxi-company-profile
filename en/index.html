Industrial Cleaning China — About Blue Star

Danyang Blue Star Cleaning — Your Trusted Chemical Cleaning Contractor in China Since 2001

China Industrial Cleaning Association Member · Founded 2001 · 25+ Years of Industrial Cleaning Excellence

Danyang Blue Star Anti-corrosion Cleaning Co., Ltd. (Blue Star Cleaning) is a leading industrial cleaning company in China, founded in 2001 and headquartered in Danyang City, Jiangsu Province, China. As a member of the China Industrial Cleaning Association (CICA), we provide professional chemical cleaning and high-pressure water jetting services with over 25 years of field experience.

As a trusted chemical cleaning contractor in China, our core heat exchanger cleaning service covers shell-and-tube, plate, spiral plate, and floating head exchangers. We also specialize in pipeline cleaning, boiler descaling, central AC maintenance, reactor cleaning, condenser cleaning, gas cooler cleaning, and evaporative condenser descaling across petrochemical, power generation, pharmaceutical, food processing, metallurgy, and commercial building industries.

Service coverage: Jiangsu (Nanjing, Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou, Zhenjiang, Yangzhou, Nantong), Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Ningbo, Huzhou, Wenzhou), Shanghai, Anhui (Hefei, Ma'anshan, Chuzhou), Shandong, Henan and nationwide. 500+ enterprise clients, 1000+ completed projects.

Our 10 core technologies include: online chemical cleaning without shutdown, cooling water system treatment, central AC water stabilization, LiBr chiller maintenance, thermal oil system decoking, HP water jetting (500-2800 bar), gas primary cooler cleaning, LiBr solution regeneration, LiBr cavity cleaning, and evaporative condenser descaling with zinc coating protection.
