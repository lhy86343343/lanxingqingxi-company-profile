Service Offerings

7 specialized cleaning services for all industrial equipment needs

🔥

Heat Exchanger Cleaning

Chemical descaling services for shell-and-tube, plate, and spiral heat exchangers. Our specialized cleaning formulations effectively remove carbonate scale, silicate scale, and bio-slime, restoring heat transfer efficiency and reducing energy costs.

Chemical Cleaning
🔩

Pipeline Cleaning

Chemical and high-pressure water jetting services for industrial pipeline systems. Covering process pipes, circulating water pipes, steam pipes, and oil pipes — effectively removing internal scaling, rust, carbon deposits, and sediments to ensure flow and process safety.

Chemical + HP Water
❄️

Central AC Cleaning

Comprehensive cleaning and maintenance for LiBr absorption chillers and freon centrifugal/screw units. Includes condenser, evaporator, absorber, and cooling tower cleaning plus water quality stabilization — improving cooling efficiency and extending equipment life.

Maintenance
🔥

Boiler Cleaning

Chemical cleaning services for industrial boilers, utility boilers, and waste heat boilers. Expert removal of carbonate, sulfate, silicate, and iron oxide scale using circulation or soak cleaning methods — restoring thermal efficiency and ensuring safe operation.

Chemical Cleaning
🏭

Gas Primary Cooler Cleaning

Specialized chemical cleaning for coke oven gas primary coolers. Targeting tar deposits, naphthalene fouling, and coal ash buildup on both tube and shell sides with custom cleaning formulations — restoring cooling efficiency for safe, stable coking operations.

Chemical Cleaning
⚗️

Reactor Cleaning

Professional chemical cleaning for glass-lined, stainless steel, and carbon steel reactor jackets and inner walls. Using safe, gentle cleaning processes to remove polymer residues, coking deposits, and crystalline scale without damaging the equipment substrate.

Chemical Cleaning
⚡

Power Plant Condenser Cleaning

High-pressure water jetting and chemical cleaning for steam turbine condensers and air coolers. 500–2800 bar HP water jets efficiently clear hard scale and blockages from condenser tubes — chemical options also available — significantly improving vacuum levels and generation efficiency.

HP Water Jetting
