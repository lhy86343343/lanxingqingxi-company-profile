Yancheng Industrial Equipment Cleaning Services

September 2, 2026

Abstract: Yancheng on the Jiangsu coast hosts automotive manufacturing, wind/solar energy equipment, steel and fine-chemical industries. Heat exchangers, boilers, thermal oil furnaces and process pipelines foul during operation, cutting heat transfer, raising energy use and sometimes forcing shutdowns. Danyang Blue Star Cleaning combines chemical cleaning with high-pressure water jetting tailored to local scale types and the coastal high-chloride environment, with dedicated passivation and corrosion protection. Acceptance follows GB/T 25146-2010; heat transfer efficiency is restored above 90% and descaling rate above 95%, with neutralized wastewater discharge and same-day on-site surveys across Yancheng.

1. Yancheng Industrial Landscape and Cleaning Demand

Yancheng is an important coastal manufacturing base in northern Jiangsu, with an industrial system built on automotive manufacturing, new-energy equipment, steel, fine chemicals, textiles and food processing. The Yancheng Economic & Technological Development Zone clusters vehicle assembly and parts plants whose painting, electro-coating and welding-cooling lines rely on many heat exchangers and cooling-water systems; the wind-power and PV equipment bases on the coast at Dafeng and Sheyang demand highly reliable large heat exchangers and thermal oil systems.

Scaling is a common operational pain across Yancheng plants: 1 mm of scale on a heat exchanger tube wall cuts heat transfer by roughly 8-10%; 3 mm of boiler scale wastes up to 25% of fuel; coked thermal oil furnaces suffer degraded heat transfer and local overheating risk. We have served Yancheng and surrounding areas for years, completing hundreds of cleaning jobs on heat exchangers, boilers, thermal oil furnaces, reactors and pipelines, and we understand the tight maintenance windows of continuous automotive painting lines and wind-power equipment production.

2. Typical Equipment and Scale Patterns in Yancheng Plants

Automotive and parts plants: pre-treatment and electro-coating circulation heat exchangers run on process water and readily form carbonate scale; air-compressor coolers and welding cooling-water systems foul mainly with rust scale and microbial slime; drying ovens and paint-line thermal oil boilers develop coking in tubes and oil circuits after several years of service.

Steel and chemical plants: blast-furnace and converter cooling-water systems and waste-heat boiler surfaces form hard silicate and sulfate scale; chemical-plant shell-and-tube heat exchangers and reactor jackets collect complex carbonate-polymer composite deposits, and some process media cause under-deposit corrosion, so cleaning plans must be tailored to materials and scale composition.

Coastal environment factors: Yancheng is close to the sea — air is salt-laden and cooling water has elevated chloride. If activated stainless steel surfaces are not passivated promptly after cleaning, chlorides can trigger pitting and stress corrosion. This is the key difference from inland cleaning jobs and is addressed specifically in our coastal-area plans.

3. Combined Chemical Cleaning and High-Pressure Water Jetting

Chemical cleaning: carbonate scale is removed by circulating Sulfamic Acid or Citric Acid; rust and mill scale by Citric Acid with a reducing agent; silicate scale with an HF auxiliary formulation under corrosion protection as needed. Every formulation is blended with BTA or MBT inhibitor: carbon steel corrosion rates are controlled per Table 2 of GB/T 25146-2010, with stainless steel and copper alloys far below the standard limits. Acceptance follows Table 3 of the standard — descaling rate no less than 95% for carbonate/rust scale and no less than 85% for sulfate/silicate scale.

High-pressure water jetting: 500-2800 bar high-pressure and ultra-high-pressure jetting units clear blocked tube bundles, strip polymer scale from reactor walls and remove mill scale from pipelines; sensitive surfaces such as glass-lined reactors are cleaned with low-pressure flat-spray nozzles to protect the lining. The two methods are often combined — chemical cleaning softens the deposit first, then jetting strips and flushes it away, the most efficient route for composite scale.

Coastal passivation: after cleaning, carbon steel surfaces are active and are passivated with Sodium Nitrite or Na₃PO₄ solution to form a protective film, then flushed with demineralized water to neutral; stainless steel passivation is followed by chloride-residue control to prevent re-corrosion in the coastal high-chloride environment. Waste liquid is collected, neutralized and settled to pH 6-9 before discharge.

4. Coverage and Response Across Yancheng

Danyang Blue Star Cleaning serves all of Yancheng — Tinghu, Yandu and Dafeng districts, Dongtai city, and Jianhu, Sheyang, Funing, Binhai and Xiangshui counties. The distance from Danyang is about 200 km; a survey team can arrive the same day a request is received, and emergency shutdowns can be expedited. 5. Yancheng Service Track Record & Cases

An automotive parts plant in Yancheng: pre-treatment heat exchangers carried carbonate scale and the temperature difference narrowed markedly, disturbing bath temperature control. Six hours of Sulfamic Acid circulation with inhibitor protection achieved about 96% descaling, heat transfer restored above 93%, the line returned to normal rhythm, saving about RMB 150,000 a year in steam.

A wind-power equipment manufacturer in Dafeng: the thermal oil boiler and oil circuits coked after four years, with local overheating and wide temperature swings. A dedicated solvent circulation plus high-pressure water jetting (800-1200 bar) removed all coke; thermal oil indices passed re-testing and the system temperature difference returned to design range.

A chemical plant in Binhai: shell-and-tube heat exchangers and reactor jackets carried carbonate-polymer composite scale with partially blocked tubes. Chemical cleaning softened the deposits, then high-pressure water jetting cleared each tube; all bundles were restored to full flow and heat exchange capacity returned to new-equipment level, with wastewater neutralized before discharge. Client information is anonymized; more Yancheng cases are available on request.

6. Cleaning Quality Standards, Safety and Environment

Yancheng projects follow one quality standard: scale and materials surveyed before work; pH, temperature, iron-ion concentration and corrosion coupon loss monitored throughout; acceptance organized per GB/T 25146-2010 Specification for Acceptance of Industrial Equipment Chemical Cleaning Quality, with corrosion data and cleaning reports delivered. Safety measures include guarded work zones and dedicated supervision, with specific plans for work at height and in confined spaces. Environmentally, waste liquid is collected and neutralized, sludge is disposed of per regulations, and the coastal environment is protected. We recommend that Yancheng plants schedule preventive cleaning every 1-2 years with routine maintenance windows; Danyang Blue Star offers inspection contracts to intervene before scaling becomes severe.

7. Yancheng Industrial Equipment Cleaning FAQ

Q1: Which equipment in Yancheng plants needs regular cleaning most?

Heat exchangers, boilers, thermal oil furnaces, reactor jackets and process pipelines. Automotive paint-line heat exchangers, wind-power plant thermal oil systems and chemical-plant shell-and-tube exchangers are the highest-frequency items — every 1-2 years alongside maintenance windows.

Q2: How does coastal cleaning differ from inland work?

The main difference is corrosion protection. Salt-laden air and elevated chloride in Yancheng cooling water mean prompt, thorough passivation and chloride-residue control after cleaning — otherwise stainless steel can pit in its activated state. We apply a dedicated passivation procedure on all coastal projects.

Q3: How many days of shutdown are needed? Will production be affected?

Small heat exchangers take 4-8 hours; large boilers and thermal oil systems take 1-3 days depending on scale and blockage. Work can be arranged in maintenance windows or night low-load periods, and online (non-stop) chemical cleaning is available to minimize downtime.

Q4: Can a coked thermal oil furnace be cleaned properly?

Yes. The coke layer is sampled and analyzed first, softened with a dedicated solvent circulation, then stripped by high-pressure water jetting. Thermal oil indices are re-tested after cleaning, and heat transfer and temperature difference return to design level.

Q5: How is pricing done in Yancheng? Can we have a survey before a quote?

Pricing is based on equipment type, size, scale thickness and form — typically a few thousand to tens of thousands RMB for heat exchangers, and tens of thousands upward for large boilers and reactors. The on-site survey and quotation are free, with no hidden fees; VAT invoices are available.
