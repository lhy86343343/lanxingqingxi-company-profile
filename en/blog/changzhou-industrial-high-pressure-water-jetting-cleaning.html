1. Industrial Equipment Cleaning Demand in Changzhou

Changzhou sits at the economic core of southern Jiangsu, with an industrial landscape anchored by smart manufacturing, chemical new materials, and photovoltaic new energy. The Xinbei Binjiang Chemical Park hosts hundreds of fine chemical and pharmaceutical intermediate enterprises, Wujin High-Tech Zone is home to extensive high-end equipment and automotive component manufacturers, and Jintan District specializes in salt chemistry and lithium battery materials. Changzhou's total industrial output exceeded 2 trillion yuan in 2025, supporting an enormous base of production equipment with continuously growing maintenance and cleaning demands.

Compounding the challenge, Changzhou's regional water supply has relatively high hardness (total hardness typically 150-250 mg/L), causing heat exchangers and piping systems to scale significantly faster than in other southern Jiangsu cities. Chemical plant reactor walls frequently accumulate polymer adhesion and crystalline residues that conventional manual hammering and low-pressure flushing cannot reach deep inside tube bundles. High-pressure water jetting has seen steady growth in Changzhou's industrial cleaning market, now established as a standard supporting service for annual chemical park turnarounds.

2. High-Pressure Water Jetting Technology Principles

2.1 Working Mechanism

High-pressure water jetting uses pure water as the working medium. A high-pressure plunger pump pressurizes water to 500-2800 bar, delivering it through high-pressure hoses to specialized nozzles that produce water jets 0.5-2.5 mm in diameter at supersonic velocities (300-900 m/s). The jet impact on scale surfaces produces four synergistic effects: Impact — the immense kinetic energy of the high-speed jet directly fractures brittle scale layers; Wedge Splitting — water penetrates scale fissures, expands, and peels deposits from within; Shear — water jets scour tangentially along tube walls, removing adhesive sludge and soft deposits; Cavitation — microbubbles collapsing in the high-pressure zone release localized shock waves (instantaneous pressures up to several thousand bar), assisting the breakdown of stubborn hard scale.

Compared with chemical cleaning, high-pressure water jetting requires no acids, alkalis, or corrosion inhibitors, generates no wastewater treatment burden, and is especially suited for chemical parks and potable water systems with strict discharge regulations. Impact force can be precisely controlled by adjusting pressure and standoff distance, ensuring safe operation on carbon steel, stainless steel, copper, and titanium substrates alike.

3. Changzhou High-Pressure Water Jetting Service Solutions

3.1 Applicable Equipment Types

Common high-pressure water jetting targets in Changzhou industrial facilities span five categories: Shell-and-tube and plate heat exchangers — removing CaCO₃ hard scale and blockages from tube interiors to restore heat transfer efficiency; Reactors and storage tanks — stripping polymer adhesion layers and crystalline residues from vessel walls to prevent cross-contamination; Industrial process pipelines — clearing wall deposits to restore design diameter and flow rate; Condensers and air coolers — cleaning inter-fin fouling and tube sheet blockages to improve vacuum; Boiler systems — removing water scale, soot, and ash deposits to boost thermal efficiency.

3.2 Process Flow

  1. Site Survey: Measure scale thickness, hardness, and distribution; determine pressure grade and optimal nozzle type
  2. Equipment Setup: Position high-pressure pump unit, water tank, hoses, and safety barriers systematically
  3. Test Run: Low-pressure trial to confirm jet direction and verify protective measures are in place
  4. Zone Cleaning: Advance zone by zone through tube bundles or vessel wall sections for complete coverage
  5. Inspection & Acceptance: Visual cleanliness check supplemented by borescope examination of tube penetration rate

3.3 Safety Assurance

High-pressure water jetting involves ultra-high energy levels where safety is the paramount priority. Operators wear ballistic-grade protective suits and face shields; the work zone is demarcated with hard barriers and prominent warning signs; high-pressure hoses undergo periodic non-destructive testing and replacement on schedule; emergency stop devices remain on standby within one-hand reach of the operator; full-time radio communication is maintained throughout the operation; solo operation is strictly prohibited.

5. Local Case Study

Project Background: A chemical enterprise in Changzhou Xinbei District operated a shell-and-tube heat exchanger (200 m² heat transfer area, 304 stainless steel tube bundle) for 18 consecutive months, after which heat transfer efficiency dropped approximately 40% with outlet temperature deviation exceeding 12°C, impacting process stability. Upon opening the channel head, CaCO₃ hard scale averaging 2.5 mm thickness was discovered on tube interior walls, with several tubes near the inlet tube sheet completely blocked.

Cleaning Approach: Scale sample analysis confirmed carbonate hard scale. A 1800 bar high-pressure water jetting system with rotary nozzles was selected for tube-by-tube cleaning. The rotary nozzle spins at approximately 3000 rpm inside each tube, producing a helical jet trajectory for full wall coverage. For completely blocked tubes, a 0° straight jet nozzle was first used to penetrate the blockage and establish a pathway, followed by the rotary nozzle for diameter restoration. Per-tube cleaning time averaged 30-40 seconds; total cleaning duration was approximately 4 hours.

Results: Post-cleaning tube penetration rate recovered to over 98%, heat transfer efficiency returned to 95% of design value, and outlet temperature deviation narrowed from 12°C to within 3°C, restoring normal production. The entire process required no chemical agents; the only effluent was scale-laden water, treatable to discharge standards after simple sedimentation.
