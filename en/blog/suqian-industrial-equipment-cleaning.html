Suqian Industrial Equipment Cleaning Services

September 2, 2026

Abstract: Suqian, the renowned "Liquor Capital of China" in northern Jiangsu, is home to the Yanghe and Shuanggou distilleries alongside textile, food and chemical industries. Distillation condensers, fermentation cooling systems, heat exchangers, boilers and process pipelines accumulate carbonate scale, organic deposits and biofilm that cut heat transfer and threaten product hygiene. Danyang Blue Star Cleaning combines chemical cleaning with high-pressure water jetting, applying food-grade safety control and corrosion protection, with acceptance per GB/T 25146-2010 across all of Suqian.

1. Suqian's Industrial Profile and Cleaning Needs

Suqian sits in the north of Jiangsu beside Hongze Lake, Luoma Lake and the Grand Canal. Known as the "Liquor Capital of China", it produces two of the country's most famous baijiu brands — Yanghe and Shuanggou — and the distilling industry, with its supporting suppliers, is the city's most distinctive industrial identity. Textiles, food and beverages, new materials and chemicals add further breadth. Breweries and food plants rely heavily on heat exchangers, condensers, boilers and process piping that run around the clock and rank high among equipment needing regular cleaning.

The cost of fouling is easy to quantify: scale on condenser walls lowers the heat transfer coefficient, raises steam use and leaves vapor under-condensed, so distillation yield fluctuates; a fouled fermentation-jacket cooling system loses temperature control and directly affects yeast metabolism and base-wine quality; every 3 mm of boiler scale wastes about 25% of fuel. We have served Suqian and the surrounding region for years, completing hundreds of cleaning jobs on heat exchangers, condensers, boilers, fermentation cooling systems and pipelines, and we are used to working inside distillery overhaul schedules and food-hygiene audit windows.

2. High-Fouling Equipment in Suqian Breweries and Food Plants

Distillation condensers: baijiu distillation condenses spirit vapor back to liquid; the cooling-water side builds carbonate scale over time while the vapor side collects spirit residue and traces of organics. Once both layers accumulate, heat exchange falls off, distillate runs hot and spirit loss rises.

Fermentation and saccharification cooling: fermentation tank jackets or coils regulate temperature with circulating water; scale slows heat transfer, making tanks hard to cool in summer and slow to warm in winter. Plate heat exchangers on saccharification vessels handle starchy liquor and trap protein and fiber deposits between plates.

Boilers and steam piping: steaming and sterilization boilers in hard-water areas develop silicate and calcium-magnesium mixed scale; scaled tubes run hot and lose efficiency. Steam mains and headers build mill scale and rust over the years that travels with the steam into process equipment.

Storage tanks and spirit lines: tartrate crystals on tank walls and bottom sediment need periodic removal, while biofilms and organic deposits inside spirit lines and circulation piping affect hygiene indicators and must be treated to food-contact standards.

3. Cleaning Routes and Food-Hygiene Control

Suqian jobs always start with scale sampling to fix the deposit type before choosing the route. Carbonate-dominated systems are cleaned by circulating Sulfamic Acid; rust and mill-scale systems switch to Citric Acid with a reducing agent; organic scale and biofilm on the product side are first emulsified with alkaline food-equipment cleaner, then acid-washed if needed. Every route carries BTA or MBT inhibitor so carbon steel corrosion stays within Table 2 of GB/T 25146-2010, with stainless steel and copper alloys far less affected.

Mechanical methods play an equal part: 500-2000 bar high-pressure water jetting clears blocked tubes, strips tank-wall deposits and scours pipe interiors. Surfaces that touch spirit or product are cleaned preferentially with plain water jetting or food-grade agents, ruling out chemical residue at the source. Acceptance follows Table 3 of GB/T 25146-2010 — descaling rate no less than 95% for carbonate/rust scale and no less than 85% for sulfate/silicate scale.

Hygiene and environment are the two hard lines on every Suqian project. Equipment that contacts product is flushed with clean or hot water to neutral after cleaning and, where required, sampled for residue before the line returns to service. Spent liquor is collected and neutralized to pH 6-9 before discharge, keeping Hongze Lake and Luoma Lake catchments untouched.

5. Local Case Records in Suqian

A baijiu distiller in Siyang: shell-and-tube condensers on the distillation section had scaled on the cooling-water side over three years — distillate ran hot and the condensate would not cool down. After scale analysis we circulated Sulfamic Acid for six hours; the tubes came through clear, condensing temperature returned to process spec and distillation speed and spirit loss normalized.

A food plant in Sucheng District: a plate heat exchanger was fouled on both sides — protein deposits on the product side and water scale on the cooling side. We worked in two stages: alkaline cleaner to emulsify the protein, thorough flushing, then acid descaling on the water side. Heat transfer recovered to above 95% of design value and the unit passed its hygiene-licence re-inspection.

A textile dyeing plant in Shuyang: the heating heat exchangers feeding dyeing vats had run for years — carbonate scale on the cooling-water side and dye-residue deposits on the liquor side made heating noticeably slower and raised energy use per batch, with dyeing temperature swings affecting colour fastness. An alkaline assistant was circulated to emulsify and flush away the dye deposits, then the water side was descaled with Sulfamic Acid. Heating speed returned to design level, per-batch dyeing time shortened and steam consumption fell about 12%. Client details are anonymized; more Suqian records are available on request.

7. Suqian Industrial Equipment Cleaning FAQ

Q1: How does cleaning distillery equipment differ from ordinary industrial work?

The difference is hygiene. Condensers, heat exchangers and piping that touch spirit or product must be rinsed free of any chemical residue so flavour and food safety are never compromised. On Suqian projects we favour food-grade agents or plain water jetting for contact surfaces, with optional residue sampling after completion.

Q2: Does jacket fouling on fermentation tanks affect fermentation?

Directly. Scaled jackets or coils transfer heat slowly, temperature control lags and the tank cannot follow the process curve, disturbing yeast metabolism and spirit flavour. Clean the jacket scale once a year during maintenance to keep heat exchange at design level.

Q3: How long is the shutdown? Can work fit the off-season?

A single heat exchanger or condenser takes 4-8 hours; boilers and full systems take 1-3 days. Work can be arranged in the overhaul or low-season window, and online (non-stop) chemical cleaning is available where conditions allow, keeping downtime to a minimum.

Q4: How is cleaning wastewater handled to stay compliant?

Spent liquor goes to a collection tank, is neutralized and settled to pH 6-9, then discharged within limits — nothing reaches the Hongze Lake or Luoma Lake catchments. Cleaning agents are biodegradable grades and settled solids are transported per regulation, with records available for environmental audits.

Q5: How is pricing handled in Suqian? Can we have a survey before a quote?

Pricing depends on equipment type, size, scale thickness and deposit form: heat exchangers typically run from a few thousand to tens of thousands RMB, while boiler and system-level projects start in the tens of thousands. Suqian clients always receive a free survey before the quotation, which covers agents, equipment, labor and waste disposal with no added charges; VAT invoices are available.
