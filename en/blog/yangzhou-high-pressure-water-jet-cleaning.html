1. Yangzhou's Industrial Cleaning Demands

Abstract: Yangzhou, a key industrial city in the Yangtze River Delta, hosts major clusters in chemicals, machinery manufacturing, cable production, and shipbuilding — all requiring rigorous equipment cleanliness standards. High-pressure water jetting, with its zero chemical residue, zero substrate damage, and rapid descaling capability, has become the preferred cleaning method for Yangzhou's industrial enterprises. This article covers typical applications, equipment specifications, and service coverage for high-pressure water jetting in Yangzhou.

1. Industrial Cleaning Needs in Yangzhou

Located at the confluence of the Yangtze River and the Grand Canal, Yangzhou achieved over 900 billion RMB in above-scale industrial output in 2024, with four 100-billion-level clusters: new chemical materials, high-end equipment, automotive parts, and advanced electrical equipment. The Yizheng Chemical Industrial Park hosts major players like Sinochem Yangnong and Far Eastern Union Petrochemical, where heat exchangers and piping systems accumulate polymer coking and carbonate scaling after prolonged operation. Gaoyou, known nationwide as the "Cable Capital," sees calcium scale buildup in cooling circuits of wire-drawing equipment across hundreds of factories. Along the Yangtze riverbank, shipbuilding and repair yards require thorough removal of welding slag, rust, and oil residues from newly assembled piping systems before delivery. In all these scenarios, high-pressure water jetting is the optimal solution.

Unlike chemical cleaning, high-pressure water jetting requires no acid agents, produces no wastewater treatment burden, and causes zero corrosion to substrate materials. Operating pressures range from 500 to 2,800 bar, adjustable for everything from gentle rinsing of precision components to aggressive removal of scale layers exceeding 10 mm.

2. Frequently Asked Questions from Plant Managers

Q1: "Half my heat exchanger tubes are clogged — can high-pressure water really clean them?"

A: Yes, and much faster than chemical cleaning. Shell-and-tube heat exchanger tubes typically have inner diameters of 19-25 mm. Our self-rotating nozzle lances are only 6-12 mm in diameter — they fit and rotate freely. At 2,800 bar, calcium carbonate scale peels off in sheets, without the 8-12 hour circulation soak that acid cleaning requires. Last year we cleaned a DN800 fixed-tube-sheet exchanger with 426 tubes for a Yizheng chemical plant — half a day of water jetting, 95%+ descaling rate, and the unit was back online that same afternoon.

Q2: "Will it damage my tubes? Stainless steel scratches easily."

A: No — pressure is fully controllable. For stainless steel tubes we use the 1,000-1,500 bar mid-pressure range with fan nozzles that spread the jet across the surface rather than focusing on a single point. For polished stainless or titanium, we drop to 500-800 bar in pure-water flush mode. In 25 years of operation, we have never had a single case of tube wall perforation.

Q3: "How do you price this? Is there a call-out fee?"

A: Fixed-price per equipment or daily rate — initial inspection is free. Small jobs (single heat exchanger, pipe section) are quoted as a lump sum after on-site assessment. Large projects (full plant shutdown cleaning) are billed by the shift — 8 hours with a two-person crew. On-site inspection within Yangzhou is free of charge. If the quote doesn't work for you, there's no obligation.

Q4: "You're based in Danyang — how fast can you respond? We can't afford downtime."

A: Danyang to central Yangzhou is 50 minutes via the Yangli Expressway; to Yizheng it's 40 minutes. We guarantee 2-hour arrival anywhere in Yangzhou. For emergencies like overnight shutdown repairs, call 18952832843 directly — we'll help assess whether you can run at reduced pressure until we arrive.

Q5: "How long does the cleaning last? Is there a warranty?"

A: 8-12 months under normal conditions, with a 6-month warranty and free rework if issues arise. After cleaning we provide operating recommendations — cooling water inhibitor dosing rates, blowdown frequency adjustments. Follow them and annual cleaning is usually sufficient.

5. Case Study

Project Background: In November 2025, a chemical enterprise in Yizheng had a U-tube heat exchanger (shell side: cooling water; tube side: organic process fluid) that lost 42% of its heat transfer efficiency after three years of operation. Inspection revealed 6-8 mm of mixed calcium carbonate and calcium sulfate hard scale on the tube exterior.

Cleaning Approach: 2,500 bar diesel-driven high-pressure pump with DN10 self-rotating multi-orifice nozzles, tube-by-tube cleaning. Chemical cleaning agent was applied to the shell-side baffle dead zones unreachable by the water jet as a supplementary measure.

Results: 97%+ descaling rate across all 186 tubes with zero wall damage. Post-cleaning heat transfer efficiency recovered to 94% of design value, saving approximately 180,000 RMB annually in steam costs. The client upgraded their cleaning strategy from reactive breakdown maintenance to scheduled annual preventive cleaning.
