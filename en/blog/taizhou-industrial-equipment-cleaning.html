Taizhou Industrial Equipment Chemical Cleaning Services

2026-07-10  |  Regional · Chemical Cleaning · Equipment Maintenance  |  Author: Danyang Blue Star Cleaning

Abstract: Taizhou, a key chemical and pharmaceutical manufacturing hub in the Yangtze River Delta, hosts the Taixing Fine Chemical Park and China Medical City (CMC). Danyang Blue Star Cleaning provides chemical cleaning and high-pressure water jetting services for heat exchangers, reactors, pipelines, and boilers across Taizhou, covering stainless steel, titanium, and glass-lined equipment with corrosion inhibition and compliant wastewater treatment.

1. Taizhou Industrial Cleaning Background

Located in central Jiangsu Province, Taizhou has developed a robust industrial base centered on fine chemicals, biomedicine, shipbuilding, and advanced materials. The Taixing Economic Development Zone is a national-level fine chemical park hosting hundreds of chemical enterprises. China Medical City (CMC) is one of China's largest pharmaceutical industry clusters, home to hundreds of drug and medical device manufacturers. Core production equipment — heat exchangers, reactors, distillation columns, and pipeline systems — inevitably accumulates scale and deposits during long-term operation.

Scale types in Taizhou reflect distinct industry characteristics: chemical plants predominantly face carbonate, sulfate, and polymer scale; pharmaceutical facilities encounter organic residues and biofilms; shipbuilding enterprises deal with metal oxide scale and welding residues. Every 1mm of scale thickness reduces heat exchange efficiency by approximately 8-10%, leading to increased energy consumption, reduced throughput, and shortened equipment lifespan.

Danyang Blue Star is located approximately 100 km from Taizhou, enabling rapid response across all districts — Hailing, Gaogang, Jiangyan, Taixing, Jingjiang, and Xinghua — with on-site inspection within 1 hour and technical proposals within 24 hours.

2. Common Equipment Cleaning Scenarios in Taizhou

Heat Exchanger Scaling: Shell-and-tube and plate heat exchangers are the most common heat exchange equipment in Taizhou chemical plants. Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ions in circulating cooling water precipitate as carbonate scale at elevated temperatures, clogging tube passes and reducing heat transfer efficiency. In severe cases, tubes become completely blocked, causing production shutdowns. Blue Star employs Sulfamic Acid or Citric Acid with high-efficiency corrosion inhibitors in a 6-8 hour circulating cleaning process, achieving over 95% scale removal and restoring heat exchange efficiency to like-new levels.

Reactor Residue: Glass-lined and stainless steel reactors in pharmaceutical and fine chemical enterprises often accumulate polymer residues, carbonized deposits, and crystalline buildup on inner walls. Glass-lined reactors cannot tolerate strong acids (which damage the glass lining) and require neutral cleaning agents or low-pressure water jetting (<500 bar). Stainless steel reactors can use an integrated pickling-passivation process that removes scale while forming a Cr₂O₃ passive film to prevent secondary corrosion.

Pipeline Blockage: Process pipelines, cooling water pipes, and steam pipes in Taizhou chemical and pharmaceutical plants develop internal scale that reduces effective diameter. A 5mm scale layer in a DN100 pipe reduces effective diameter to 90mm, cutting flow by approximately 19%. Blue Star uses a combined chemical cleaning + high-pressure water jetting (500-1500 bar) approach — chemical softening followed by hydraulic stripping — to restore design flow capacity.

Boiler Descaling: Industrial and thermal oil boilers in the Taixing and Jingjiang areas, fed with hard water, are prone to silicate and iron oxide scale. A 3mm boiler scale layer can waste up to 25% of fuel. Blue Star applies specialized boiler descaling agents in circulating cleaning, supplemented by high-pressure water flushing of convection tube bundles, restoring thermal efficiency to over 90%.

3. Core Cleaning Technologies

Chemical Cleaning Process: Cleaning agent selection is based on scale sample analysis — Sulfamic Acid for carbonate scale (low metal corrosivity), HF-assisted formulations for silicate scale, Citric Acid with reducing agents for iron oxide scale. All formulations include BTA or MBT corrosion inhibitors, with carbon steel corrosion rates controlled below 6 g/m²·h, copper tubes below 0.3 g/m²·h, and zero corrosion on stainless steel and titanium. Cleaning solution circulation is maintained at 60-80°C, flow velocity 0.5-1.0 m/s, with pH and iron ion concentration sampled every 30 minutes for dynamic parameter adjustment.

High-Pressure Water Jetting: Equipment rated 500-2800 bar is deployed with pressure and nozzle selection based on material and scale hardness — fan nozzles at low pressure (<500 bar) for glass-lined reactors, rotary nozzles at medium-high pressure (1000-1500 bar) for carbon steel pipelines, and diamond nozzles at ultra-high pressure (2000-2800 bar) for stubborn coke deposits. This purely physical method uses zero chemical additives, with effluent consisting only of dislodged scale and water.

Passivation Treatment: After chemical cleaning, carbon steel equipment surfaces are in an activated state and must undergo passivation. Sodium Nitrite or Na₃PO₄ passivation solution forms a dense Fe₃O₄ protective film with a uniform steel-gray or blue-black appearance, preventing secondary rusting. Equipment is flushed with demineralized water to neutral pH within 24 hours post-passivation.

4. Taizhou Clients & Case Studies

Blue Star has served multiple enterprises across Taizhou's chemical, pharmaceutical, food, and manufacturing sectors:

Taixing Fine Chemical Enterprise: Eight shell-and-tube heat exchangers with severe scaling — shell-side temperature differential had dropped from the design 15°C to under 5°C, reducing capacity by 40%. On-site scale analysis identified a calcium carbonate + calcium sulfate mixed scale. A Sulfamic Acid + Citric Acid composite formulation with BTA inhibitor was applied in a 6-hour circulating clean. All tubes were fully unblocked, temperature differential restored to 14°C, heat exchange efficiency recovered to 93%. Neutralized wastewater was discharged at pH 6-9 compliance. The client saves approximately ¥300,000 annually in electricity costs.

CMC Pharmaceutical Enterprise: Three 5000L glass-lined reactors with polymer residue and crystalline buildup 3-5mm thick on inner walls, affecting heat transfer and agitation efficiency. Since glass lining cannot tolerate strong acids, a neutral surfactant soak + low-pressure fan water jet (400 bar) alternating process was used, completely removing all residue while fully preserving the glass lining. Post-cleaning heat transfer coefficient returned to 96% of new equipment specification.

Jingjiang Shipbuilding Supplier: DN150-DN300 pipeline systems with internal scale + oxide scale affecting hydraulic and cooling system operation. Citric Acid circulating cleaning for 12 hours removed oxide scale, followed by high-pressure water jetting (1200 bar) to strip residual deposits, and NaNO₂ passivation to form a protective film. Pipeline pressure loss returned to normal, cooling efficiency improved by 30%.
