1. Suzhou's Industrial Landscape and Cleaning Demands

1. Suzhou Industrial Overview and Equipment Cleaning Demands

Suzhou is the prefecture-level city with the largest industrial volume in the Yangtze River Delta region, with above-scale industrial output exceeding ¥4.5 trillion in 2025. The city hosts four leading industries: electronic information, equipment manufacturing, biomedicine, and advanced materials. Suzhou Industrial Park and Suzhou New District, two national-level development zones, concentrate large numbers of foreign-invested and high-end manufacturing enterprises covering sub-sectors including semiconductors, precision machinery, chemical pharmaceuticals, and food processing. This vast industrial system means enormous quantities of heat exchangers, boilers, central air conditioning systems, and process pipelines operate continuously — and the scaling and corrosion issues affecting these devices directly impact enterprise energy efficiency and production safety.

Suzhou lies within the Taihu Lake basin where both surface water and groundwater have moderate Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ion content (total hardness approximately 120–180 mg/L), classifying it as a medium-hardness water zone. In circulating cooling water systems, as concentration cycles increase, sparingly soluble salts such as CaCO₃ and CaSO₄ precipitate and deposit on heat exchange surfaces, forming dense scale layers. Additionally, Suzhou's summers are hot and humid (July–August average temperatures above 32°C, relative humidity 80%), imposing heavy operating loads on cooling towers and chiller units with rapid water quality deterioration — equipment fouling rates are significantly higher than during autumn and winter.

2. Common Equipment Cleaning Issues for Suzhou Enterprises

2.1 Heat Exchanger Fouling and Efficiency Degradation

Shell-and-tube and plate heat exchangers in Suzhou's electronics and chemical enterprises commonly exhibit CaCO₃ water scale deposits on heat transfer surfaces due to long-term use of medium-hardness circulating cooling water. Typical scale thickness is 0.5–3 mm, with increased thermal resistance causing 15%–30% heat transfer efficiency decline. Semiconductor factory ultra-pure water cooling systems demand extremely high heat exchanger cleanliness; even trace scaling can compromise process temperature control precision.

2.2 Central AC System Scale and Biological Contamination

Suzhou has dense concentrations of commercial complexes, office buildings, and hotels with large-scale central air conditioning systems. Cooling towers, chronically exposed to ambient air, accumulate dust and microorganisms on fill material. During summer high-temperature conditions, the risk of algae and Legionella proliferation increases significantly. Condenser copper tube inner-wall scaling in lithium bromide absorption chillers and Freon chiller units leads to reduced cooling efficiency and increased energy consumption.

2.3 Industrial Boiler and Steam System Scaling

Suzhou's textile dyeing and finishing, chemical, and food enterprises extensively use steam boilers and thermal oil boilers. Under the high-temperature operating conditions of boilers, CaCO₃ and silicate scale from Taihu basin water deposits more rapidly. Measured data shows that low-pressure steam boilers without water treatment can accumulate 1–2 mm of scale per quarter, with annual thermal efficiency decline reaching 5%–8% and corresponding fuel cost increases. Particularly noteworthy is the extensive use of thermal oil boilers in Suzhou's textile dyeing industry: thermal oil undergoes thermal cracking and oxidative polymerization after prolonged high-temperature operation, generating carbon deposit layers on tube inner walls with thermal conductivity of only 0.1–0.2 W/(m·K) — less than one-thousandth that of steel tubing. In severe cases this leads to tube wall overheating deformation and even tube rupture incidents.

2.4 Pipeline and Reactor Cleaning Maintenance

Suzhou's pharmaceutical and fine chemical enterprises impose extremely stringent cleanliness requirements on process pipelines and reactors. Scale and corrosion products in reactor jackets not only affect heat transfer efficiency but can become product quality hazards — one Suzhou pharmaceutical enterprise experienced product batch-to-batch purity fluctuations exceeding 2 percentage points due to reactor jacket scaling causing uneven heating. For GMP-certified enterprises, equipment cleaning records are critical audit items during equipment qualification and periodic requalification, with cleaning protocols, operational records, and efficacy verification reports forming a complete compliance evidence chain. Additionally, stainless steel pipeline systems in Suzhou food processing enterprises, after long-term transport of sugar- and fat-containing materials, develop organic scale layers on inner walls that not only impair heat exchange but serve as breeding grounds for microorganisms, requiring periodic CIP cleaning and sanitization treatment.

7. Suzhou Industrial Equipment Cleaning FAQ

Q1: How often should heat exchangers in Suzhou be cleaned?

With medium-hard water and hot, humid summers, Suzhou heat exchangers typically lose 15%–30% of heat transfer efficiency after 6–12 months, so annual cleaning is generally recommended. Semiconductor and biomedical plants with high cleanliness requirements should inspect every six months, watching the temperature difference and pressure drop.

Q2: How do I choose between online no-shutdown cleaning and shutdown deep cleaning?

Continuous-production plants choose online no-shutdown cleaning — ambient-temperature, low-pressure chemical cleaning through the circulation loop works while equipment runs, ideal for Suzhou electronics and pharmaceutical plants (zero downtime). During shutdown maintenance, deep cleaning is chosen and can be combined with high-pressure water jetting. In the case above, online cleaning achieved 98% scale removal and 96% efficiency recovery.

Q3: How is thermal oil boiler coking handled?

Thermal oil cracks and oxidizes under prolonged high temperature, forming a carbon layer on tube walls with thermal conductivity of only 0.1–0.2 W/(m·K) — under one thousandth of steel. A dedicated organic coke remover circulates at 160–180°C to dissolve the carbon layer, assisted by high-pressure water jetting, preventing tube wall overheating, deformation and even tube rupture.

Q4: Can pharmaceutical reactor cleaning satisfy GMP audit requirements?

Yes. For GMP-certified plants, cleaning records are a key item in equipment qualification and requalification audits. We provide complete cleaning plans, operation records and effectiveness verification reports — including scale analysis, corrosion rates and heat transfer efficiency comparisons — forming a compliant evidence chain that meets GMP and ISO audit requirements.

Q5: How is the waste cleaning solution handled? Will it pass environmental inspection?

Waste liquids are collected, neutralized and discharged per environmental standards, strictly following GB/T 25146-2010 acceptance criteria, with complete cleaning reports and coupon corrosion data kept on file, compliant with Suzhou environmental authority requirements.
