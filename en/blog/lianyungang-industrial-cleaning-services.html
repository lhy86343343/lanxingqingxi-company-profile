Lianyungang Industrial Equipment Cleaning Services

2026-07-21 · Regional Service · Lianyungang  |  Author: Lanxing Qingxi

Abstract: Lianyungang, as the eastern bridgehead of the New Eurasian Land Bridge and a national-level petrochemical base, hosts major industrial clusters in petrochemicals (Shenghong Petrochemical, Satellite Chemical), pharmaceuticals (Hengrui, Chia Tai Tianqing, Kanion, Hansoh), carbon fiber (Zhongfu Shenying), and wind power equipment manufacturing. Industrial equipment in these sectors faces severe scaling and corrosion challenges. Lanxing Qingxi provides chemical cleaning and high-pressure water jetting services across all Lianyungang districts — Xinpu, Haizhou, Lianyun, Ganyu, Donghai, Guanyun, and Guannan — with 24-hour on-site response.

1. Lianyungang Industrial Profile & Cleaning Needs

Lianyungang is a major port-based industrial city in Jiangsu Province. The Xuwei New District National Petrochemical Base hosts large-scale refining and chemical plants where heat exchangers and condensers face severe CaCO₃ scaling and process-fluid coking. The coastal high-humidity environment accelerates external corrosion. The pharmaceutical cluster — China's largest — requires GMP-compliant reactor and pipeline cleaning without chloride-containing agents. Carbon fiber production involves high-temperature furnace tube decoking, while wind power equipment requires gearbox oil circuit cleaning. The marine climate also promotes rapid microbial growth in circulating cooling water systems, making cooling tower and condenser tube maintenance critical.

3. Technical Solutions

3.1 Petrochemical Equipment

For composite fouling in petrochemical heat exchangers, a Sulfamic Acid (8-10%) + Citric Acid (3-5%) system with BTA+Urotropine inhibitor is circulated at 55-65°C for 4-6 hours. Heavily oil-coked equipment receives alkaline Surfactant pre-treatment (Na₂CO₃+NP-10) before acid descaling. Corrosion rates controlled to ≤4.0 g/(m²·h) for carbon steel and ≤1.0 g/(m²·h) for stainless steel.

3.2 Pharmaceutical GMP Cleaning

Pharmaceutical reactors and process piping are cleaned with Citric Acid (5-8%) as the primary agent (chloride-free formula) with specialized stainless steel inhibitor. Post-cleaning conductivity testing and riboflavin coverage verification are performed. All cleaning records comply with GMP audit traceability requirements.

3.3 HP Water Jetting

Hard external tube bundle scale and carbon fiber furnace deposits are removed with 500-700 bar high-pressure water jetting. Combined with chemical cleaning, descaling rates exceed 95%.

5. Local Case Studies

Case 1 — Petrochemical Heat Exchanger: A U-tube heat exchanger (350 m²) at a Xuwei petrochemical plant experienced a 28°C outlet temperature drop after 2 years. Tube bundles showed 3-5 mm oil-coke composite scale. Alkaline degreasing followed by Sulfamic Acid circulation for 8 hours restored outlet temperature to design value, saving ~350,000 RMB/year in steam costs.

Case 2 — Pharmaceutical Reactor: A 3000L glass-lined reactor at a Lianyungang ETDZ pharmaceutical plant lost 40% jacket cooling efficiency. Citric Acid (6%) + Surfactant cleaning at 50-55°C for 5 hours restored jacket heat transfer to 93% of design with zero glass-lining damage.
