Abstract: Wuhu is a national advanced manufacturing base with three dense industrial clusters: automotive (Chery vehicles and parts), home appliances (Midea air-conditioner and compressor plants) and building materials (Conch Group cement production lines). Heat exchangers, compressor units, paint-shop circulation systems and waste-heat boilers run around the clock, making scale, fouling and bio-slime problems acute. This article reviews the common cleaning scenarios, technologies and service process for industrial equipment in Wuhu.

1. Why Wuhu Industry Needs Regular Equipment Cleaning

Wuhu has a strong industrial base: the Chery-led automotive industry covers vehicles, engines and components; the Midea Wuhu base runs air-conditioner and compressor lines around the clock; and Conch Group, headquartered in Wuhu, demands high reliability from cement kiln systems and waste-heat recovery units. One thing these three industries share is fast scaling in cooling water, heat exchange and thermal systems.

Located on the Yangtze River, Wuhu has hot, humid summers. Cooling water evaporates and concentrates quickly, promoting scale and bio-slime. Fouled heat exchangers lose efficiency sharply — workshops lose cooling in summer, equipment trips, and paint-shop heat exchangers destabilize bath temperature, dragging down first-pass paint quality. More companies now schedule preventive cleaning instead of waiting for breakdowns.

We have served Wuhu and surrounding areas for years, completing hundreds of chemical cleaning and high-pressure water jetting jobs on heat exchangers, central AC, pipelines, boilers and reactors across Jinghu, Yijiang, Jiujiang, Wanzhi, Fanchang, Nanling and Wuwei.

2. Common Equipment Cleaning Scenarios in Wuhu

Automotive manufacturing: paint-shop circulation heat exchangers and spray systems are the top cleaning target — bath temperature must stay within process range, and fouling slows heating, hurting film gloss and adhesion. Welding-shop cooling jackets and fixture cooling lines, once blocked, cause weld temperature drift; injection and die-casting mold cooling channels are narrow, and one scale plug means a pile of rejects.

Home appliance production: compressor units, condenser coils, workshop central AC cooling towers and fan coil units accumulate scale and dust over time, lowering cooling capacity until summer workshop temperatures cannot be controlled.

Cement and building materials: kiln waste-heat boilers (AQC/SP) and clinker cooler tube banks run in dusty flue gas; heavy ash and scale deposits reduce boiler output and power generation and require shutdown cleaning. Chemical plants and food factories also face various scale types in pipelines and reactors.

In short, Wuhu demand concentrates on four equipment categories: heat exchangers, compressor units and central AC, pipelines and boilers, and fine mold/cooling water circuits — each with a tailored approach.

3. Core Cleaning Technologies

Chemical cleaning: for scale, rust and bio-slime, we use circulation cleaning with LX-903 (sulfamic-acid-series) or LX-900 series (organic-acid) compound cleaners as the main agent, with dedicated corrosion inhibitors and dispersants, online or during shutdown. Dedicated copper inhibitors protect copper-alloy equipment. Chemical cleaning removes scale thoroughly, reaching inside complex tube bundles — the first choice for heat exchangers and condensers.

High-pressure water jetting: for hard scale, coke, polymer deposits and mechanical blockages, 500–2800 bar water jets physically break scale without chemicals and without damaging the base material — ideal for pipelines, reactors, condenser tube banks and waste-heat boiler tube rows.

Passivation pre-filming: after chemical cleaning, carbon steel equipment is neutralized, passivated and pre-filmed to form a protective layer that slows re-corrosion and re-scaling — the step many companies overlook but which decides how long the cleaning benefit lasts.

All work follows GB/T 25146-2010 Industrial Equipment Chemical Cleaning Quality Standard, with descaling rate, corrosion rate and passivation film quality verified item by item. Waste liquid is neutralized and treated; heavy-metal or organic waste is handed to licensed disposal units with full documentation for environmental compliance.

5. Service Process for Wuhu Customers

Step 1 — free on-site survey: our engineers visit to review equipment type, scale form, operating conditions and shutdown windows, and give an initial assessment.

Step 2 — sampling, analysis and quotation: scale samples are analyzed to determine composition, then a cleaning plan and quotation are issued with schedule, acceptance criteria and safety/environmental measures.

Step 3 — execution: chemicals and equipment are mobilized per plan; chemical cleaning runs in closed circulation, HP water jetting zones are guarded, and the whole process is documented with photos.

Step 4 — acceptance and follow-up: acceptance per GB/T 25146-2010, then regular follow-up visits to track equipment condition for the next service cycle. Response time for Wuhu and nearby customers is within 24 hours; emergency shutdowns can be expedited.

6. Wuhu Cleaning FAQ

Q1: How often should our heat exchangers be cleaned? What if we do not clean them?

Usually once or twice a year depending on water quality and duty. Yangtze River water is hard and evaporates fast in summer, so a pre-summer clean is wise. If temperature difference has grown, energy consumption is rising, or pressure drop has increased, scale is already thick — schedule cleaning soon. Without cleaning, efficiency keeps falling, energy costs climb, and in severe cases tubes block and the unit is forced offline — a far bigger loss than the cleaning cost.

Q2: Paint-shop heat exchanger fouling — does it really affect paint quality?

Yes, visibly. Unstable bath temperature hurts film flow and adhesion, and first-pass quality drops. We have cleaned such exchangers for local vehicle plants; once temperature stabilizes, pass rates return to normal.

Q3: Will high-pressure water jetting damage our equipment?

No. The jet removes scale, not the base metal, and pressure is set per equipment type and scale hardness — low pressure where needed. We have established parameters for thin-wall tube bundles and fins, with no base-metal damage in years of work; wall thickness is checked at acceptance.

Q4: How long does cleaning take offline? Our line cannot stop?

Most jobs fit within shutdown windows — night shifts, weekends or maintenance periods. Chemical cleaning runs in closed circulation, and some cooling water systems can be cleaned online while running. After the survey we give a schedule that does not interrupt production.

Q5: Do you have a local crew in Wuhu? How is pricing done?

We keep crews stationed in Anhui; a survey team can reach Wuhu within 24 hours. Pricing is based on equipment type, quantity, scale form and method — survey first, then a quotation covering chemicals, labor, equipment and waste disposal, with no surprise add-ons.
