1. Overview of Nanjing Industrial Heat Exchanger Cleaning Demands

1. Overview of Nanjing Industrial Heat Exchanger Cleaning Demands

As a key industrial city in the Yangtze River Delta, Nanjing hosts multiple large industrial clusters including the Jiangbei New Materials Science and Technology Park, Nanjing Chemical Industrial Park, and Jiangning Economic and Technological Development Zone — with dense concentrations of chemical, power, steel, and pharmaceutical industries maintaining an enormous installed base of heat exchangers. The Yangtze River water in the Nanjing section has relatively high hardness, with pronounced scaling tendencies in circulating cooling water systems. Heat exchangers typically experience 20%–40% heat transfer efficiency decline after 6–12 months of operation, making regular chemical cleaning an essential maintenance measure for ensuring efficient production unit operation.

The company is a member of the China Industrial Cleaning Association with 25 years of industry experience, having cumulatively served over 100 enterprises in Nanjing and surrounding areas.

2. Common Heat Exchanger Issues in Nanjing

Affected by the Yangtze River water quality, heat exchanger scaling in Nanjing is predominantly CaCO₃ water scale, with some chemical installations experiencing accompanying silicate scale and iron oxide deposits. Common issues include: shell-and-tube heat exchanger tube bundle internal and external wall scaling causing heat transfer efficiency decline, plate heat exchanger inter-plate flow channel blockage causing increased pressure differential, and spiral-plate heat exchanger weld zone stress corrosion. Differentiated chemical cleaning solutions must be developed based on scale types and equipment types.

With Nanjing's hot and humid summers and cold winters, heat exchanger cleaning windows typically concentrate during the March–May and October–November annual maintenance seasons. Blue Star Cleaning supports year-round emergency response, including holidays.

3. Heat Exchanger Chemical Cleaning Technical Solutions

The following cleaning solutions are adopted for mainstream heat exchanger types in Nanjing:

Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchangers: HCl-series or LX-903 (sulfamic-acid-series compound cleaner) circulation cleaning, combined with dedicated corrosion inhibitors and surfactant penetrants. For stainless steel tube bundles, LX-903 systems are preferred to avoid Cl⁻ stress corrosion cracking risks. Alkali neutralization and nitrite-series passivation are performed after cleaning.

Plate Heat Exchangers: After disassembly, LX-900 series (organic-acid compound cleaner) soak cleaning is employed, combined with molybdate-series corrosion inhibition protection. LX-900 series offers high safety for stainless steel plate surfaces and can complex iron oxides; simple water flushing after cleaning is sufficient without additional neutralization steps.

Spiral-Plate Heat Exchangers: LX-903 (sulfamic-acid-series compound cleaner) + LX-900 series (organic-acid compound cleaner) composite formulation circulation cleaning is used, with a dedicated copper corrosion inhibitor, focusing on protecting spiral body weld zones. Cleaning flow velocity is controlled at 0.5–1.0 m/s to ensure uniform scale stripping within flow channels.

5. Nanjing Service Case Study

Case: Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger Cleaning for a Nanjing Chemical Enterprise

Equipment parameters: Carbon steel shell-and-tube heat exchanger, heat exchange area 180 m², tube-side medium circulating cooling water, shell-side medium organic solvent vapor. After 14 months of operation, outlet temperature deviated 18°C from the design value, with tube bundle inner wall scale thickness of 2–4 mm.

Cleaning solution: HCl-series cleaning agent + dedicated corrosion inhibitor + surfactant formulation, circulation cleaning temperature 55–65°C, cleaning duration 5 hours. Pre-cleaning coupon test corrosion rate: 1.5 g/(m²·h), inhibition efficiency 96.2%, exceeding national standards. Post-cleaning heat exchange efficiency recovered to 94% of design value, steam consumption reduced by 17%, yielding annual energy cost savings of approximately ¥210,000.
