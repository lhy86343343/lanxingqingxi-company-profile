Core Technologies

Independent R&D + Industry-Leading, Meeting Complex Operating Conditions

10 Core Technologies Overview

Independent R&D + Industry-Leading, Meeting Complex Operating Conditions

01

Online Chemical Cleaning at Normal Temperature & Pressure

Complete chemical cleaning while equipment operates normally — no shutdown or cooldown required. Using proprietary chemical cleaning formulations and circulation cleaning processes, effectively removes scale without disrupting production, significantly reducing downtime losses. Applicable to online cleaning of all types of heat exchangers, pipelines, and process equipment — one of our core technologies.

02

Circulating Cooling Water System Online Cleaning

Chemical cleaning, pre-filming, and water treatment technology for industrial circulating cooling water systems. Through scientific cleaning agent formulations and water quality stabilization solutions, effectively removes system scaling and microorganisms, establishes protective film layers, and extends equipment service life. Significant energy-saving results, ensuring long-term stable operation of cooling systems.

03

Central AC Water Quality Stabilization Treatment

Chemical cleaning and water quality stabilization treatment for chilled water and cooling water systems. Through a complete workflow of water quality analysis, chemical dosing, and online monitoring, effectively controls scale deposition, metal corrosion, and microbial growth, ensuring efficient and stable operation of central air conditioning systems while reducing energy consumption.

04

LiBr Absorption Chiller Maintenance

Professional maintenance technology for lithium bromide absorption chillers, covering comprehensive services including internal cavity cleaning, solution regeneration, vacuum maintenance, and component repair/replacement. Effectively extends unit service life, improves cooling efficiency, and reduces operating costs. Long-term maintenance services have been provided to numerous large enterprises.

05

Thermal Oil Heater Decoking Technology

Specialized decoking cleaning technology for thermal oil boilers and pipeline systems. Targeting carbon deposits, coking, and polymer buildup from long-term thermal oil operation, uses proprietary decoking agents and circulation cleaning processes to thoroughly clear pipeline blockages and restore heat transfer efficiency. Effectively prevents thermal oil overheating, deterioration, and equipment safety hazards caused by line blockage.

06

High-Pressure Water Jetting Technology

500-2,800 bar high-pressure water jetting technology, equipped with multiple models of high-pressure pumps and specialized nozzles. Uses the immense impact force of ultra-high-pressure water jets to effectively remove various stubborn deposits, coke scale, polymers, cement scale, and sediments. Suitable for physical cleaning of heat exchanger tube sides, pipe interiors, reactor jackets and other equipment — safe, environmentally friendly, with zero secondary pollution.

07

Gas Primary Cooler Cleaning Technology

Professional chemical cleaning technology and construction processes for coke oven gas primary coolers in coking plants. Addresses the unique challenges of tar deposits, naphthalene scaling, and coal dust accumulation, with proven safe, efficient, and thorough cleaning procedures and techniques. Multiple coking plant gas cooler cleaning projects completed, accumulating rich engineering experience to ensure efficient and safe equipment operation.

08

LiBr Solution Regeneration Technology

Testing, analysis, performance adjustment, and regeneration treatment technology for lithium bromide solutions. Using professional equipment to regenerate LiBr solutions whose performance has degraded during operation, restoring solution concentration and performance indicators. Substantially reduces new solution replacement costs, extends unit service life, with significant economic and environmental benefits.

09

LiBr Chiller Internal Cavity Cleaning

Specialized internal cavity cleaning technology for lithium bromide absorption chillers. Addresses corrosion products, deposits, and contaminants from long-term operation, using proprietary cleaning processes to thoroughly clean all cavities — generator, absorber, evaporator, condenser — restoring heat and mass transfer performance, ensuring unit vacuum integrity and cooling efficiency.

10

Evaporative Condenser Descaling

Specialized cleaning technology for evaporative condensers. Targets scale buildup on condenser coil exteriors, bio-slime, and fill media deposits using combined chemical cleaning + high-pressure water jetting. Copper tube corrosion inhibitor protects heat exchange bundles, achieving >95% scale removal and restoring heat transfer efficiency to 90%+ of design. Suitable for ammonia and Freon refrigeration system evaporative condensers.
