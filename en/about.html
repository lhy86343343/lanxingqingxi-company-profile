About Us

Working Together, Creating the Future

Founded in 2001 and headquartered in Danbei Town, Danyang City, Jiangsu Province, Danyang Blue Star Anti-corrosion Cleaning Co., Ltd. is a specialized service enterprise integrating industrial equipment cleaning, anti-corrosion treatment, technical consulting, and cleaning product R&D and sales. We are a proud member of the China Industrial Cleaning Association, with extensive engineering experience and technical expertise.

Since our inception, we have adhered to the philosophy of "Quality First, Customer Foremost," deeply serving the chemical, power generation, pharmaceutical, metallurgical, and food processing industries. We provide professional, efficient, and safe cleaning solutions for all types of industrial equipment. With a dedicated technical team, advanced cleaning equipment, and a rigorous quality management system, we create maximum value for every client.

After twenty years of growth, Blue Star Cleaning has become a leading regional industrial equipment cleaning service provider. Our business covers Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui, Shandong, Shanghai, and beyond. We have established long-term, stable partnerships with numerous renowned enterprises. Our 10 core cleaning technologies span chemical cleaning, high-pressure water jetting, online cleaning without shutdown, and other advanced processes — capable of meeting equipment cleaning needs under the most complex operating conditions.

Looking ahead, Blue Star Cleaning will continue to uphold the development philosophy of "Working Together, Creating the Future," constantly improving our technical capabilities and service standards to deliver superior industrial equipment cleaning services and contribute to the high-quality development of Chinese industry.
